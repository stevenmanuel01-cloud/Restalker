Restalker Mobile 1.0 (Android) - first version.

- Watches a few Amazon products on its own, all day (a notification stays while it watches).
- Buys a product set to Buy when it is in stock at or under your cap, with the desktop Restalker's own Amazon checkout.
- One purchase per product; Practice mode (on at first) stops every checkout before "Place your order".
- Restock messages with Buy now / Snooze 3 h; a purchase is told only after it was found in your Amazon orders.
- Never answers a robot check; never types a password or card number.

Install: open this file on your Android phone and allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser when Android asks.
